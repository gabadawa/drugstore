DrugStore Easy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

DrugStore Easy is a web application for managing drug-store inventory and related store operations.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, the application may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile information made available through Google Sign-In.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to authenticate users, identify their account, provide access to the application, and maintain the security of the service.

Google User Data

DrugStore Easy does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not used for advertising. Information obtained through Google authentication is used only for the functionality described above and is handled in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to application data is limited to authorized users and system functions.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and rights of users and the service.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements. Users may contact us to request information about their account or request deletion where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.
